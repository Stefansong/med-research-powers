# Revision Response

Plan the revision strategy and draft a point-by-point response to reviewers.

Invoke the `revision-response` skill, which contains the authoritative revision strategy (triage major/minor, conflicting-reviewer handling, what to concede vs defend) and the point-by-point response-letter format. Pass through the user's reviewer comments / decision letter: $ARGUMENTS

The skill produces a structured rebuttal: per-comment response, the exact manuscript change made, and the location of each change. For handling many comments in parallel, see `/team-collaboration`.
